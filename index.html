QuitsUp Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Your data stays on your device

QuitsUp keeps your Groups, people, expenses, receipts, settlements and loans on your device. There is no QuitsUp account. QuitsUp has no server that receives your data unless you choose to share a Group with another phone across iPhone and Android (see Sharing a Group with another phone). A name you save as My Name in Settings stays on your device and is only used to suggest your name when you create a Group or choose This is me. The person you pick as yourself in a Group you have joined (Which person are you?) is kept on your phone only: it is not sent to the Owner, to the Group's other members or to any server, and it is not part of the Group.

Shared Groups

If you share a Group, its records are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. The people you share it with can see that Group, and Editors can change it. Everything saved in the Group is visible to them: expenses, receipts, a place recorded on an expense, a payment line you chose to show, and a Budget the Owner sets. The developer of QuitsUp cannot read any of it. Groups you do not share never leave your device unless you turn on Sync my devices. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

If you are the Owner of a shared Group, your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account find it in your iCloud and show the same shared Group. This happens whether or not Sync my devices is on, because the Group already lives in your iCloud. When you copy a shared Group, QuitsUp invites the same people with the same roles, and each person must accept.

Sharing a Group with another phone (iPhone or Android)

If you choose to share a Group with another phone, including an Android phone, QuitsUp encrypts the Group on your phone before it leaves your phone, and the keys stay on the phones. The encrypted copy is stored on a server run by the QuitsUp developer, hosted by Cloudflare, so the other phones can fetch it. The people you share it with can see that Group, and Editors can change it. Everything saved in the Group is visible to them: expenses, receipts, a place recorded on an expense, a payment line you chose to show, and a Budget the Owner sets.

The server does see an anonymous list of the Group's devices with their public keys and roles, which phone connects and when, and the number, size and time of the stored records. Its hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes each request and keeps standard request logs, such as the network address, the time and the size of a request, for a limited time under its own policy. To limit how many shared Groups one network can create, the server also keeps the network address (for IPv6, its first 48 bits) and the time of each Group creation, and clears entries older than two days when the next Group is created. There is no account or sign-in. Receipt photos are removed from the server once every device has them, and after 7 days at most. A Group's encrypted copy is removed after 90 days without use (30 days if nobody has joined it), or when you stop sharing or delete the Group. Each phone keeps its own permanent copy, so you can keep using QuitsUp offline. Sharing with iCloud, and everything you do without sharing, is unchanged.

When the owner stops sharing or deletes a shared Group, the server keeps a short record for up to 30 days so that members' phones can show that sharing ended: the Group's server id, a fingerprint of each member device's sharing credential, and the owner's signed stop request. When a member is removed, the server keeps that device's credential fingerprint for up to 30 days. When a member leaves, the server keeps a fingerprint of their credential and their signed leave notice until the owner's phone has processed it, or until the Group is removed. These records hold no names, amounts, notes or photos. When a Group ends, the server sends one last wake-up with no content to the members' phones, then deletes their push tokens.

If the Shared Group changes setting in QuitsUp Settings is on (it is on until you turn it off, whether or not you have allowed iOS notifications), your phone also gives the sharing server its Apple push token, once for each Group you share this way, so the server can send a short message that tells your phone to check for changes. The message names no Group and carries no names or amounts. The server therefore holds your phone's push token, linked to the Group's anonymous device list, and the same token in two Groups lets the server see that both belong to the same phone. Apple's push service delivers the message and learns that your phone received a message at that time. The token expires 30 days after QuitsUp last renewed it (QuitsUp renews it about once a week), and the server deletes an expired token the next time that Group's list is used or changed. The token is also removed when you leave or stop sharing a Group, turn the notification setting off, or Apple reports the token is no longer valid. An app you have force-quit, or have not opened since restarting your phone, may not receive these messages until you open it once.

Location

The first time you open QuitsUp on a device, it shows a short explanation of what notifications and location are used for, and then asks iOS for permission, once. If you allow location, QuitsUp also turns on location tagging. The explanation has a single Continue button, after which iOS shows its own permission dialogs, where you choose to allow or not allow. You can turn tagging off at any time with Use my location in QuitsUp Settings, and you can change the permission in iOS Settings. If you had already turned it off, QuitsUp does not turn it back on.

When location is on, QuitsUp reads your location only while you use the app, one reading at a time, and never in the background. It uses it to suggest the currency of the country you are in and to tag an expense, loan or repayment with where it happened. Tagging never changes a balance, and QuitsUp works without it.

The coordinates and the time of the reading are saved in that record on your device, and are never sent to the developer. Because the record is part of your data, they travel with it when it is stored in iCloud: in a shared Group, the people in the Group can receive them and the Spending Map shows where members recorded expenses, and with Sync my devices on they reach your other devices through your own iCloud.

To suggest a currency, to show a place name and to draw the Spending Map, QuitsUp uses Apple's location lookup service, which receives the recorded coordinates. This goes to Apple, never to the developer.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts, such as balance reminders, budget notices and updates when someone changes a shared Group, are created on your device. QuitsUp has no server that sends them. For shared Groups, Apple's iCloud tells QuitsUp that a Group changed, and QuitsUp then shows the notice on your device. For Groups shared with another phone across iPhone and Android, QuitsUp checks the sharing server from time to time, including in the background when iOS allows it, or when a content-free wake-up from the sharing server asks it to (see Sharing a Group with another phone), and shows the notice on your device. You can turn them off in iOS Settings and in QuitsUp Settings.

Nearby Join

When you add a friend to a Group nearby, or join one, QuitsUp uses your device's local network to find the other phone and connect directly to it. You approve the Local Network permission the first time. The phones compare a short code and the connection is encrypted. Only the invitation you approve is exchanged: your name as typed on the joining screen, the host's name, the Group's name after approval, and the invitation: the iCloud invitation link, or, for a Group shared across iPhone and Android, the details the other phone needs to join it. The Group's records themselves then go through the sharing server, as described under Sharing a Group with another phone, and not over the local network. Nothing else is sent, nothing is shared with the developer, and nothing is advertised unless the Nearby screen is open.

Your money (Personal Money)

Income, cash exchanges and exchange rates you record in Personal Money stay on your device. They are included in your backups, are never added to a Group or shared with anyone, and are not sent to the developer. Money Flow, which shows these figures month by month, is worked out on your device from the same records. If you turn on Sync my devices in Settings (it is off until you do), they are also kept in your own private iCloud account through Apple CloudKit so your own devices show the same figures. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read them, and the developer cannot. Turn the switch off and nothing more is sent. Personal expenses you record and the monthly personal budget you set are kept the same way as the rest of Personal Money. If you choose Move to Group on a personal expense, it becomes part of that Group and is visible to its members like any other expense; Move to Personal takes an expense you paid alone back into your own records. If you turn on the Personal Money lock, iOS checks your Face ID or passcode; QuitsUp never receives your biometric data.

Payment details

Payment details you add in Settings stay on your phone and are added to reminders you send. If you turn on Show to Group members, that one line is also saved in your shared Groups' iCloud space so the people in those Groups can see how to pay you. Everyone in those Groups can read it. It is off until you turn it on, turning it off removes it, and it is never sent to the developer.

Budgets

A Budget you set for a Group you do not share stays on your device. It is included in your backups, is not sent to the developer, and its notifications are local to your device.

If you are the Owner of a shared Group and set a Budget, its amount, currency and end date are saved with that Group in your iCloud so every member sees what is left. Only the Owner can change it, and clearing it clears it for everyone. How much has been spent is worked out on each member's own device, and each member's notifications are local. The developer cannot read it.

Purchases (QuitsUp Plus)

QuitsUp is free to use. QuitsUp Plus is optional and is sold as Yearly (with a free trial), Lifetime, Lifetime Family, and a Trip Pass for one Group. QuitsUp Duo, a higher tier, adds sharing Groups with Android phones and is sold as Yearly (with a free trial). The App Store shows the prices and takes the payment.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple, through your Apple Account. QuitsUp never sees or stores your card or payment details, and it has no account of its own. QuitsUp learns what you have bought from Apple on your device and keeps a note of it on the device so Plus keeps working offline. Settings has Restore Purchases, and Manage Subscription for a Yearly plan, which Apple manages. People in your Apple Family Sharing group can use Lifetime Family; Apple manages who is in the family, and QuitsUp does not see who they are. A Trip Pass is bought for one Group, and an anonymous code for that Group, not its name or contents, is attached to the Apple purchase so the pass can be matched to it again.

Camera, receipts and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt, take a Group cover photo, or scan an invitation QR code to join a Group. The text on a receipt is read on your device, and receipt images are not uploaded to the developer or to any other service, except as part of a Group you share with another phone, where they are encrypted first (see Sharing a Group with another phone). Receipt images stay on your device, or in a shared Group if you add them to one, or in your own iCloud if you turn on Sync my devices. A cover photo has its location data removed on your device.

Search (Spotlight) and Siri

If you leave Show in Spotlight search on in QuitsUp Settings (it is on until you turn it off), QuitsUp adds your Groups and their expenses to the search index on your device, so you can find them from the iPhone search screen. A Group result carries the Group's name and category. An expense result carries its title, amount and currency, its date and the Group's name. QuitsUp never adds Personal Money, income, exchanges, loans, payment details, receipts, locations, the names of people in a Group or the Example Group. This search index is kept on your device by iOS and is managed by Apple. QuitsUp does not send it to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not part of QuitsUp backups or of Sync my devices. Search results, including amounts, can be seen by anyone who can use search on your unlocked phone, and on the Lock Screen if you allow search results there in iOS Settings. Turn the switch off and QuitsUp removes everything it added at once. Deleting, archiving or leaving a Group, or voiding an expense, removes its results too.

QuitsUp keeps a short record on your device of whether Siri or Shortcuts reached it: counts and outcomes only, never an amount, a title or a name. It stays on your device unless you copy it yourself from Settings and share it. Deleting the app removes it. Voice questions (who owes whom, budget left, amount spent) are answered on your device from your own data, and Siri may speak a Group name or amount aloud, including while your phone is locked if your iOS setting allows it (Face ID & Passcode > Allow access when locked > Siri). Personal Money is never included.

Reference exchange rates

When you open the Exchange Rate sheet, QuitsUp may request a reference rate from Frankfurter using the two currency codes and selected date. The request does not include names, amounts or Group identifiers. Frankfurter and network intermediaries may receive connection information such as your IP address. You can enter a rate without a connection.

No tracking

QuitsUp does not track you. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data.

Updates

To tell you about updates, QuitsUp asks the App Store for the latest version number. Nothing about you or your Groups is sent.

Deleting your data

Delete a Group in QuitsUp to remove it and its records. Deleting the app removes all QuitsUp data from your device. Leaving a shared Group removes your access to it, and your copy stays on your phone until you delete it. Leaving a Group shared across phones also sends a signed leave notice to the sharing server. Stopping sharing a Group across phones deletes its encrypted copy from the sharing server (receipt photos are queued for deletion and removed shortly afterwards), and the short records described under Sharing a Group with another phone stay for up to 30 days. Deleting the app also removes the search results QuitsUp added to your device.
