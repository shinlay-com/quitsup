QuitsUp Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Your data stays on your device

QuitsUp keeps your Groups, people, expenses, receipts, settlements and loans on your iPhone. There is no QuitsUp account, and QuitsUp has no server that receives your data.

Shared Groups

If you share a Group, its records are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit, and the people you share it with can see and change that Group. The developer of QuitsUp cannot read them. Groups you do not share never leave your device. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Location

Location is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, QuitsUp uses your location only while you use the app, to suggest a currency and to tag an expense with where it happened. It is never used in the background, and you can turn it off at any time. The Spending Map and an expense's place name use Apple's location lookup, which sends the recorded coordinates to Apple and never to the developer.

Nearby Join

When you add a friend to a Group nearby, or join one, QuitsUp uses your device's local network to find the other phone and connect directly to it. You approve the Local Network permission the first time. The phones compare a short code and the connection is encrypted. Only the invitation you approve is exchanged: your name as typed on the joining screen, the host's name, the Group's name after approval, and the iCloud invitation link. Nothing else is sent, nothing is shared with the developer, and nothing is advertised unless the Nearby screen is open.

Your money (Personal Money)

Income, cash exchanges and exchange rates you record in Personal Money stay on your device. They are included in your backups, are never added to a Group or shared with anyone, and are not sent to the developer. If you turn on Sync my devices in Settings (it is off until you do), they are also kept in your own private iCloud account through Apple CloudKit so your own devices show the same figures. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read them, and the developer cannot. Turn the switch off and nothing more is sent.

A budget you set for a Group stays on your phone. It is included in your backups, is never shared with Group members or sent to the developer, and its notifications are local to your phone.

Camera, receipts and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt. Receipt images stay on your device, or in a shared Group if you add them to one.

Reference exchange rates

When you open the Exchange Rate sheet, QuitsUp may request a reference rate from Frankfurter using the two currency codes and selected date. The request does not include names, amounts or Group identifiers. Frankfurter and network intermediaries may receive connection information such as your IP address. You can enter a rate without a connection.

No tracking

QuitsUp does not track you. It has no analytics and no advertising.

Deleting your data

Delete a Group in QuitsUp to remove it and its records. Deleting the app removes all QuitsUp data from your device. Leaving a shared Group removes your access to it.
