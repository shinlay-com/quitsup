QuitsUp for Android Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Your data stays on your phone

QuitsUp keeps your Groups, people, expenses, receipts, settlements, loans, budgets and personal money on your phone. There is no QuitsUp account, and QuitsUp has no server that receives your data. The developer does not receive, store or see your data.

Shared Groups

Sharing a Group with other people is not in this version of QuitsUp, so no Group leaves your phone. If a later version adds it, this policy will say what is stored and where before that version is released. When you share a result, a statement, a reminder or a backup file yourself, Android's share sheet hands it to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

What leaves your phone

QuitsUp sends nothing to the developer. QuitsUp itself makes one network request, and only when you open the Exchange Rate sheet for an expense or loan in a currency other than the Group's settlement currency: it asks the public exchange-rate service Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev, European Central Bank reference rates) for a suggested rate. The request contains two currency codes and a date. It contains no amounts, names, Group or expense identifiers or account, because there is none. Because it is an internet request, the exchange-rate service and the networks in between can see your phone's IP address and the time of the request. That is the only information about you that this request exposes, and nothing else about you is sent. QuitsUp does not send it to the developer. QuitsUp never does this in the background or when the app starts. A suggestion changes nothing until you tap Apply and Save, and you can type a rate without a connection. A rate fetched earlier for the same currencies and date is kept on your phone (at most 200) and still shown offline.

Location

The first time you open QuitsUp, it shows a short explanation of what notifications and location are used for, and then asks Android for permission, once. If you allow location, QuitsUp also turns on location tagging. You can turn tagging off at any time with Use my location in QuitsUp Settings, and you can change the permission in Android Settings. If you had already turned it off, QuitsUp does not turn it back on.

When location is on, QuitsUp reads your approximate location only while the app is open, one reading at a time, and never in the background. It uses it to suggest the currency of the country you are in and to tag an expense, loan or repayment with where it happened. Tagging never changes a balance, and QuitsUp works without it.

QuitsUp does not collect your location. The coordinates and the time of the reading are saved in that record on your phone and are not sent to the developer or to any QuitsUp server. Location is optional and stays off unless you turn it on. To read the location, QuitsUp uses Google Play services on your phone. To find the country, QuitsUp uses Android's built-in geocoder, which on most phones is provided by Google and may receive the coordinates; the result is a country code and nothing else is kept from that lookup. Google's own privacy policy applies to those services. When sharing a Group with other people arrives in a later version, this policy and the app's Google Play data safety information will be updated before that version is released.

Spending Map

The Spending Map is drawn on your phone from country outlines and a city-name list that are part of the app (public-domain Natural Earth data). It shows only the places already saved with your expenses. No map service or map tiles are used and nothing is sent.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts, such as balance reminders, an evening summary, frozen-settlement reminders and budget notices, are created on your phone. QuitsUp has no server that sends them. QuitsUp also listens for the phone finishing a restart, only to plan its reminders again. You can turn them off in Android Settings and in QuitsUp Settings.

Your money (Personal Money)

Income, cash exchanges and exchange rates you record in Personal Money stay on your phone. They are included in your backups, are never added to a Group or shared with anyone, and are not sent to the developer. Money Flow, which shows these figures month by month, is worked out on your phone from the same records. If you turn on the Personal Money lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock; QuitsUp never receives your biometric data.

Payment details

Payment details you add in Settings stay on your phone and are added to reminders you send. They are never sent to the developer.

Budgets

A Budget you set for a Group stays on your phone. It is included in your backups, is not sent to the developer, and its notifications are local to your phone.

Purchases

QuitsUp is free to use. This version has no purchases, subscriptions or payments, and QuitsUp never sees or stores a card or payment details.

Camera, receipts and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt or take a Group cover photo. Photos from your gallery use the Android photo picker, which gives QuitsUp only the photo you pick. The text on a receipt is read on your phone with Google's ML Kit text recognition, whose recognition model is part of the app. Receipt images and the text read from them are not uploaded to the developer or to any other service. Receipt images and the text recognised from them are processed on your phone only and never leave it. Google's SDKs (ML Kit and Google Play services) collect diagnostics data: as described by Google, this is information about how the library ran, such as device model and Android version, app name and version, an installation identifier that is not meant to identify you, and timing, image size and error codes, and never your images, your text or your Groups. This data is collected for app functionality and analytics, is sent to Google over an encrypted connection, is handled by Google as a service provider for the app, and cannot be turned off in QuitsUp. QuitsUp does not receive it. See developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure. Receipt images stay in the app's private storage on your phone. Removing a receipt image keeps its text unless you delete the expense.

Contacts

When you choose a name from your contacts for a person in a Group, QuitsUp uses Android's contact picker. QuitsUp does not hold the Contacts permission and receives only the name you pick.

Backups and exports

A backup, a CSV file or a PDF statement is a file you create in QuitsUp and save or share where you choose. A backup holds all your Groups, so keep it somewhere private. QuitsUp does not back up to any cloud on its own: its data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. Android may carry the app's data to a new phone when you set one up with a cable or a device-to-device transfer; that stays between your own devices.

Widgets

Home-screen widgets show a Group's balance from a small file on your phone. While the phone is locked, widgets hide amounts and names.

No tracking, no ads

QuitsUp does not track you. It has no analytics of its own, no advertising, no sale of data and no advertising identifier. The only data leaving your phone is described under What leaves your phone, Location and Camera, receipts and photos.

Updates

QuitsUp does not check for updates itself. Google Play delivers updates, and nothing about you or your Groups is sent by QuitsUp for that.

Children

QuitsUp is not directed to children, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete a Group in QuitsUp to remove it and its records. Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings > Apps > QuitsUp > Storage > Clear data, removes all QuitsUp data from your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing for the developer to delete on request.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what leaves the phone, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
